# Privacy Policy — YT Music

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

YT Music is an unofficial, open-source YouTube Music client. It has **no servers and no accounts of its own**, and the developer collects **no data** about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your library: liked songs, playlists, listening history, settings.
- Offline downloads, stored encrypted and readable only by the app.
- Your YouTube sign-in session, if you sign in. It is encrypted, and its key is kept in your operating system's secure store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain).
- "Made for you" recommendations, which are computed locally from your history and likes.

None of this is uploaded anywhere by the app.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects directly from your device to:

| Service | Why |
|---|---|
| YouTube / YouTube Music (Google) | search, music metadata, audio streams, and your library if you sign in |
| [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) | song lyrics (only the song title, artist, album and duration are sent) |
| GitHub | checking for and downloading app updates |
| Discord (local app on your computer) | showing "Listening to" in your status, only if enabled, and only the current song |

Those services handle requests under their own privacy policies. The app sends no analytics, telemetry or crash reports.

## Your control

- Discord status, notifications and hotkeys can be turned off in **Settings → Integrations**.
- Signing out removes the stored YouTube session.
- Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.
